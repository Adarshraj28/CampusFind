Features

Built for real campus operations

Everything a university lost & found desk needs — without the paperwork.

Smart Match Score

A transparent, rule-based score across category, description, colour, brand, location and date — never black-box, never an auto-approval.

Verified claims

Five verification questions, reviewer notes and a required rejection reason keep every decision accountable.

Chain of custody

Every movement — reported, received, stored, approved, handed over — is stamped with actor, time and location.

QR case tags

Each found item gets a printable QR code that opens the live case record for security staff on any device.

Instant notifications

Match found, claim under review, approved, rejected or ready for pickup — users always know the next step.

Admin analytics

Recovery rate, reports by category and location, audit logs and a live testing dashboard for the QA course.

About

One place for everything a campus loses — and gets back.

CampusFind is the lost & found platform for Galgotias University. Students, faculty and staff report what they lose, the campus team logs what they find, and the system scores every pair transparently before a staff member makes the call. Nothing is auto-approved, nothing is hidden: every movement through a case is written into an auditable chain of custody.

  • Role-based accessProtected pages check authentication and role on load — hidden buttons are never the only barrier.
  • Private by defaultSerial numbers, unique marks and personal contents never appear on public cards.
  • Testable by designValidation, matching, state transitions and authorisation are pure functions that ship with unit tests.
  • Accessible & responsiveSemantic HTML, keyboard navigation, ARIA labels and mobile-first layouts.
