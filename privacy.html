Privacy policy

How CampusFind handles the information of students, faculty and staff.

Last updated: —

CampusFind is operated by Galgotias University, Greater Noida. This page explains exactly what is stored, why, and how to have it removed.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, university email address, roll number, role, department and phone number.
  • Report details — the item you lost or found: description, category, colour, brand, location, date and any photos you attach.
  • Claim details — your answers to the five verification questions, and the decision the reviewing staff member reached.
  • Activity records — sign-in times and an audit trail of actions taken by staff and administrators.

Why we collect it

Only to operate the lost and found service: to match a lost item with a found one, to verify that a claimant is the real owner, to arrange handover, and to keep a defensible record of who held an item and when.

What is public and what is not

  • Public: item name, category, description, colour, brand, where and when it was lost or found, and its public case number.
  • Not public: your email address, phone number, identity documents, serial numbers, and the private notes staff attach to an item. These are shown only to you and to authorised staff.
  • Never shown to another student: who filed a report, who submitted a claim, or any private identifying detail.

Automated matching

When you submit a report, the app compares it against existing found items using a scoring algorithm covering description, category, location, date, colour and brand. This is a computer calculation, not a human judgement, and it never makes a decision about ownership — a staff member always does.

How long we keep it

Reports and claims are retained for 12 months from the date the item was returned or closed, after which they are deleted. Audit records of staff actions are retained for the same period.

Where your data is stored

Records are held in the CampusFind database operated by the university. Item photographs are stored separately in protected object storage and are never served over an unencrypted connection.

Passwords

We never store your password. A one-way hash is kept instead, so nobody — including university staff — can read your password. If you forget it, you must reset it rather than recover it.

Your rights

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask for a correction if any detail is wrong.
  • Ask for your account and its reports to be deleted.
  • Withdraw a claim you submitted.

To exercise any of these, contact the campus lost & found desk or the University's data protection officer.

Cookies and local storage

This application stores your theme preference and your session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It uses no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Changes to this policy

Any change will be published on this page with an updated date. Continuing to use CampusFind after a change means you accept the updated policy.
